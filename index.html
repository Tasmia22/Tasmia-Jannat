Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Earlier research in hyperspectral imaging and computer vision.

Before my current systems research, my MSc and BSc work focused on hyperspectral image classification, deep neural networks, transfer learning, dimensionality reduction, and spectral-spatial feature learning.

MSc Research · Transfer Learning

Cross-Domain Transfer Learning for Hyperspectral Image Classification

Investigated how ImageNet-pretrained models can be adapted to hyperspectral data despite the mismatch between RGB input and high-dimensional spectral imagery. The work modified VGG16 and ResNet50 to process multi-channel hyperspectral inputs and evaluated the approach on Indian Pines and Salinas datasets.

VGG16ResNet50Transfer LearningHyperspectral Imaging

3D Deep Learning

3D CNN with Mish Activation for Hyperspectral Classification

Explored 3D convolutional networks that jointly learn spectral and spatial information from hyperspectral data, with Mish activation used in the proposed classification approach.
